AI Assistant Widget Demo

Every element below uses the same class (the selector in dev-configs.ts) and picks its entry point with data-entry. The launcher, prompt box and search bar share one conversation and one chat window; the embedded overview has its own conversation.

launcher

A button that opens the chat drawer. This is the entry used when data-entry is omitted.

prompt-box

An "Ask AI" panel for product pages. data-pid is optional: leave it empty for no product context.

search-bar

Camera, microphone and Ask AI buttons that open the same conversation in a full-screen chat.

embedded

An AI overview of data-query with its own separate conversation. "See Results" opens its own full-screen chat.
